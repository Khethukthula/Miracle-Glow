Our Story

About Miracle & Glow

Where every bloom and every flame tells a story of love

How it started

Born from a Passion for Beauty

Miracle & Glow started as a small idea — to bring beauty, warmth, and a little bit of magic into people's everyday lives. What began as a love for fresh flowers and fragrant spaces grew into something far more meaningful.

Every bouquet we assemble and every candle we pour is done by hand, with care and intention. We don't rush the process because we believe the best things take time and heart.

What drives us

Handcrafted with Intention

Our mission is simple: to create products that feel like a warm hug. Whether you're treating yourself or surprising someone special, we want every Miracle & Glow experience to feel personal and meaningful.

We source the freshest roses and use only natural ingredients in our candles — no shortcuts, no compromises. Just pure, heartfelt craft from Pretoria, South Africa.
